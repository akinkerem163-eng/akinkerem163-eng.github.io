Why Saray Is a Must-Visit for Copper Craftsmen Lovers

There are places in Turkey that time seems to have forgotten, and Saray is one of them. Walking through its streets felt like stepping into a living museum.

What makes Saray special when it comes to copper craftsmen is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life. this is absolutely worth your time.

The copper craftsmen of Saray reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

If you are interested in copper craftsmen, Saray should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

I spoke with several locals about copper craftsmen in Saray, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

As I left Saray, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
