Why Saray Is a Must-Visit for Artisan Workshops Lovers

The artisan workshops of Saray reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here. I have shared this with literally everyone I know.

I documented my entire artisan workshops experience in Saray through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

As I left Saray, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
